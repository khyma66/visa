# News source review — November 4, 2026

Latest News uses The Texas Tribune's immigration RSS feed, including up to six
pages, to select up to 30 original stories from the preceding 90 days. Dates are
preserved and the display explicitly describes the rolling window. A smaller
edition is valid when fewer eligible stories exist; no filler is generated.

Permission: https://www.texastribune.org/republishing-guidelines/
Crawler guidance: https://www.texastribune.org/robots.txt
Feed: https://www.texastribune.org/topics/immigration/feed/

The reviewed rules permit free republication with author/publisher credit and
source links, but exclude third-party partner articles. Photos must accompany
their original stories and retain credits. Selling stories or selling ads
against those stories is prohibited. Keep this edition outside paid access and
review any future advertising placement against the publisher's current terms.
Publisher removal/correction requests must be handled promptly.

The collector verifies the reviewed terms digest before collection and checks
each article's republication control. It excludes known partner bylines and
unverified or wire-service photos. Previews use publisher-supplied teasers,
without copying full articles, inferring legal advice, or adding tracking pixels.
The reviewed robots file disallows the republication-pixel query, which this
collector does not request. It contains no restriction on these feed/article
paths. Robots permission alone is not a copyright licence; both were reviewed.

Daily collection is scheduled at 13:37 UTC (07:37 CST / 08:37 CDT). Requests are
sequential and spaced, with fixed allowed hosts, size/time limits, duplicate
protection and an overall six-feed-page cap. Upstream failure preserves the
previous edition and fails the workflow. New permissions are not assumed when
the policy digest changes. The site labels an edition stale after 36 hours.

This source review documents the implementation basis, not a guarantee against
all legal claims. Additional publishers require their own permission review;
an RSS feed, search listing or publicly readable page alone is insufficient.
